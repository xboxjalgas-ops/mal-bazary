# Мал Базары — Market Gate

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

## Brief
1. Vibe: authentic, rural, dependable, warm, quietly cinematic.
2. Journey: direct trade promise → the market opens → three simple actions → useful product breadth → publish the first listing.
3. Energy: calm hero, anticipation at the gate, clear rhythm through the steps, confident close.
4. Feeling curve: trust → curiosity → control → usefulness → commitment.
5. Signature move: the livestock market is literally revealed by a pair of timber gates opening under scroll.
6. Range: premium-minimal with rural material character, not luxury gloss.
7. Structure: distinct scenes, not one continuous world.
8. Assets: existing real-photo SVG assets and the current brand system.

## Peak
“The site where the market gate opens and all seven livestock categories are already waiting behind it.” Peak: livestock category act.

## Tell-someone sentence
It's the site where scrolling opens the livestock market gate.

## Journey and score
| Beat | Feeling | Device | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct trade | Trust | layered parallax hero | Establish real landscape and direct-sale promise |
| Market opens | Curiosity | sticky split-gate reveal | Bespoke transformation exposes the actual categories |
| Simple process | Control | scroll focus sequence | Each step takes focus without hiding the others |
| Product range | Usefulness | flow + stagger | Calm information after the peak |
| Publish | Commitment | resolved CTA | One action and a held final statement |

## Grammar and fingerprint
Grammar: chaptered editorial adapted to a practical marketplace. The landing has distinct scenes and a resolved close; it avoids a video one-shot, dashboard chrome, continuous-world flight, poster typography, gallery-first structure, split comparison, and rhythmic short-cut repetition.

Fingerprint: first registered Scrollcraft build in this repository. Distinctive dimensions: chaptered marketplace journey, sticky utility nav, layered photographic hero, split-gate peak, resolved listing CTA, bespoke timber-gate reveal.
